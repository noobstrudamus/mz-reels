## Who I am

- **Name:** M&Z Consulting
- **Handle:** @mzconsulting.llc
- **What I do, in one sentence:** We find what's wrong in a product's CPSC compliance file
  (certificate, test reports, labels) and fix it, so a blocked listing or held shipment can move.
- **Who I am talking to:** US importers and private-label brands selling on Amazon, Walmart or
  TikTok Shop; overseas factories selling into the US; customs brokers and law firms (white-label).
  The person reading a Seller Central notice or a broker's email about a held container.
- **What I sell:** free 24-hour gap list (forward the notice, get what's wrong, what fixes it and a
  fixed price) and the free CPSC Finder; paid Unblock Sprint, First-Pass File, Compliance On File,
  white-label for brokers and law firms.

## My positions

1. A passing lab report is not a certificate.
2. Most blocks are a paperwork gap, not a product failure.
3. Seller Support template answers rarely tell you which field is wrong; the rule text does.
4. On imports, the importer certifies, not the factory.
