## Caption
Is 16 CFR part 1307 on your kids' product certificate?
Under 16 CFR 1307.3, children's toys and child care articles can't be made for sale, offered for sale, distributed or imported with more than 0.1 percent of eight listed phthalates, including DEHP, DBP and DINP.
It applies to plasticized component parts too, like soft vinyl.
