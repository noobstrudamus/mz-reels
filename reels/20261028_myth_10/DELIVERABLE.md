## Caption
"The parts are certified" doesn't mean the finished toy is.
Under 16 CFR 1110.19, component part certificates are voluntary. A finished product certifier may rely on them to issue its own certificate.
Component certificates are not eFiled at entry. The finished product certificate is the one that's required (16 CFR 1110.5).
Keep both: the parts certificates and your own.
