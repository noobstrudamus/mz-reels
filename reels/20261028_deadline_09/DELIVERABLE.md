## Caption
Water bead toys have had their own section of the toy rule since March 12, 2026.
16 CFR 1250.4 adds performance and labeling requirements for water bead toys and toys containing water beads, on top of the toy standard in 1250.2, including a size test for the beads.
A certificate written before that date may cite only 1250.2.
