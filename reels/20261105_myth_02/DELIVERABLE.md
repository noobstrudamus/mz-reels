## Caption
Selling adult hoodies? The clothing flammability rule still covers them.
16 CFR part 1610 sets flammability tests for clothing textiles, with a few exceptions like most hats, short gloves and footwear.
Plain surface fabrics of 2.6 oz per square yard or more, and fabrics made entirely of acrylic, modacrylic, nylon, olefin, polyester or wool, are exempt from testing to support guaranties.
A raised-fiber cotton fleece isn't on that list, so check how the fabric was tested.
