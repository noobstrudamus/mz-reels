## Caption
Selling dressers for adults? The CPSC tip-over rule still covers them.
16 CFR part 1261 applies to any free-standing clothing storage unit made after September 1, 2023, typical of bedroom furniture, not only kids' furniture.
It points to ASTM F2057-23 and aims to protect kids up to 72 months old from dresser tip-over.
Your GCC must list part 1261 separately (16 CFR 1110.11(a)(2)).
