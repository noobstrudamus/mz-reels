## Caption
For most imports, July 8, 2026 moved certificate data from a folder to the customs entry.
Under 16 CFR 1110.13(a)(1), the certifier of an imported product eFiles the certificate data elements in ACE when the entry is filed. On imports, that's usually the importer.
Those elements come from the certificate itself: the product ID, each rule, the test lab and date, a records contact and more.
Mail imports go a different route: the data goes into CPSC's Product Registry before arrival.
Check the certificate before the entry, not after.
