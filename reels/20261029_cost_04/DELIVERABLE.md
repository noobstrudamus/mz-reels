## Caption
Does your children's product certificate say who keeps the records?
Under 16 CFR 1110.11(a)(4), each finished product certificate names the person who maintains the supporting records for the certifier, with street address, email and phone.
A position title can work, if the position is always staffed and responds to CPSC's requests.
A blank there is a gap, even when every test passed.
