## Caption
Small parcels, under de minimis, so no certificate needed? If your product needs a certificate, the rule says otherwise.
16 CFR 1110.13(a)(1) names finished products eligible for the de minimis duty exemption: the certifier must eFile the certificate data elements at the time the entry is filed.
For finished products made outside the US and imported by mail, the data goes into CPSC's Product Registry before the product arrives.
Say a seller ships in small parcels and never built the certificate data. That's the first gap.
