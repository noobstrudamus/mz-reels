16 CFR 1461.2 changed on October 19, 2026.
It now says CPSC does not read "flammable liquid fuels" in the portable fuel container definition to include fuel additives meant only to improve engine performance, clean engine parts, extend engine life or stabilize fuel.
Other rules can still apply, so she checks the product before she lists it.
