## Caption
Five products on one certificate? That's one of the quietest gaps in a CPSC file.
Under 16 CFR 1110.13(a), each finished product certificate must describe only one product.
Five products usually means five certificates, each with its own product identifier, like a SKU or UPC.
Check that each CPSC certificate, a children's product certificate or a GCC, names one product and its own identifier.
