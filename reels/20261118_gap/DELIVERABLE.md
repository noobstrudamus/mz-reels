## Caption
One certificate covering your whole product line? That's a gap.
16 CFR 1110.13(a) says a finished product certificate must accompany each product or shipment that has to be certified, and each certificate must describe only one product.
Say a seller sends a buyer one PDF listing five products. Splitting it into one certificate per product is usually the fix.
