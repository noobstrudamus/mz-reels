## Caption
"The factory keeps all that" is not a records plan.
Under 16 CFR 1110.17, certifiers keep certificates and the test records behind them for at least five years from the certificate creation date.
On imports, the certifier is usually the importer (16 CFR 1110.7(a)).
If the test records behind your children's product certificate live only with the factory, that's a gap worth closing.
