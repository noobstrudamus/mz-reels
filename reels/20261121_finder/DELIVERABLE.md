## Caption
"It's certified," says the factory. Then the PDF turns out to be a general certificate backed by an in-house test.
For a children's product, 16 CFR 1107.20(a) has manufacturers send samples to a third party conformity assessment body for testing to support certification.
And the certificate for a children's product is a Children's Product Certificate (CPC), not a General Certificate of Conformity (16 CFR 1110.3).
Say a seller only has the factory's GCC for kids' backpacks. Checking which rules apply is the first step.
