## Caption
No test needed doesn't mean the rule comes off the certificate.
Under 16 CFR 1110.11(c), when a certifier claims a statutory or regulatory testing exclusion, the certificate still lists every applicable rule.
For that rule, it identifies the exclusion in place of the date and place of testing.
Leaving the rule off is a gap, even when no test was required.
