## Caption
Neck floats for young kids got their own section of the US toy rule on June 15, 2026.
16 CFR 1250.5 adds performance and labeling requirements for neck floats, on top of the toy rule's requirements in 1250.1 and 1250.2. It covers neck floats for children up to 4 years old, used in sinks, baths and pools.
The float and its packaging carry a set warning, in English, on the principal display panel.
A certificate written before June may cite only the toy standard.
