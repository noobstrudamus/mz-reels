Coin battery inside? That's usually a rule your certificate has to list.
16 CFR part 1263 (Reese's Law) sets performance and labeling requirements for consumer products with button cell or coin batteries, to keep the batteries away from kids.
Exceptions exist, like toys that meet the battery rules in part 1250, and zinc-air cells.
