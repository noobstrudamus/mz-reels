## Caption
Think lead paint rules only cover the paint on your walls? Painted toys are in there too.
16 CFR 1303.1(a) declares toys and other articles intended for use by children that bear lead-containing paint to be banned hazardous products.
Lead-containing paint here means lead content above 0.009 percent by weight of the total nonvolatile content of the paint, or of the dried paint film.
Say a seller never checked which rules cover a painted toy. That check comes before the order.
