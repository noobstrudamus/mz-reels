## Caption
"The factory says it's certified." Here's why that's usually not the end of it.
For a product made outside the US, 16 CFR 1110.7(a) says the importer is the finished product certifier, unless a specific rule says otherwise.
So the children's product certificate should name the importer of record (usually you), plus the product, each rule, where and when it was made, the lab and test date, and a records contact.
Say a seller only has the factory's PDF. That's the first gap to close.
