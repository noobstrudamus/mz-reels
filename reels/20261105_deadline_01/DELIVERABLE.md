## Caption
Wood stove importers: one CPSC paperwork step ended on September 21, 2026.
16 CFR 1406.5, which had manufacturers and importers send CPSC copies of the stove notice and directions, was removed.
The consumer side stays: 16 CFR 1406.4 still requires a notice on the appliance with minimum clearances, floor protection and chimney type, plus directions.
Say a seller stops the mailing. The label on the stove still has to be right.
