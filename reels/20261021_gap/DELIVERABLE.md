# Reel 20261021_gap — "Gap of the week": certificate names the wrong certifier / missing fields

## Caption
Your factory sent a certificate. Whose name is on it as the certifier?
A passing lab report won't fix what the certificate itself is missing.
On imports, the certifier is usually the importer of record.
The cert also needs the certifier's full address, email and phone, plus when and where it was made, among other fields.

## Sources (current eCFR, Title 16 up to date as of 2026-10-19; Part 1110 as amended by the eFiling rule, 90 FR 1843)
| claim | source | verbatim quote |
|---|---|---|
| On imports the importer is the certifier (unless a specific rule says otherwise) | [16 CFR 1110.7(a)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1110/section-1110.7) | "…the importer, as defined in this part, is the finished product certifier that must issue a finished product certificate…" |
| "Importer" = importer of record | [16 CFR 1110.3(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1110/section-1110.3) | "Importer, for the purposes of this part, means the Importer of Record (IOR)…" |
| Certifier must be identified with full contact details | [16 CFR 1110.11(a)(3)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1110/section-1110.11) | "…including the party's name, street address, city, state or province, country or administrative region, electronic mail (email) address, and telephone number." |
| Date (month/year) and place of manufacture required | [16 CFR 1110.11(a)(5)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1110/section-1110.11) | "Provide the date (month and year, at a minimum) and place … where the finished product(s) were manufactured, produced, or assembled." |
| Rules cover both CPC and GCC (reel names neither) | [16 CFR 1110.3(b)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/part-1110/section-1110.3) | "There are two types of finished product certificates: Children's Product Certificates (CPCs) and General Certificates of Conformity (GCCs)." |
