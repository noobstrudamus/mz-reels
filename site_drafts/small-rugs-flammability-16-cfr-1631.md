# Small rugs and CPSC: what 16 CFR part 1631 covers

Rugs feel like home decor, not regulated products. But small carpets and rugs have their own federal flammability standard.

16 CFR 1631.2(a): "This Standard provides a test method to determine the surface flammability of small carpets and rugs when exposed to a standard small source of ignition under carefully prescribed draft-protected conditions. It is applicable to all types of small carpets and rugs used as floor covering materials regardless of their method of fabrication or whether they are made of natural or synthetic fibers or films, or combinations of, or substitutes for these."

"Small" has a definition in 16 CFR 1631.1(c): no dimension greater than 1.83 m (6 ft) and an area not greater than 2.23 m² (24 sq ft).

## What that means for the paperwork

- 16 CFR 1110.5: products subject to a rule enforced by the Commission that are imported or distributed in commerce "must be accompanied by a finished product certificate."
- For a small rug sold for general use, that is a General Certificate of Conformity (GCC), defined in 16 CFR 1110.3.
- 16 CFR 1110.11(a)(2): the certificate "must identify separately all applicable rules." A small-rug GCC that skips part 1631 has a gap.
